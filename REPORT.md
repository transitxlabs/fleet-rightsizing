TransitX Labs Inc. maintains a public-data work sample demonstrating typed ingestion, row reconciliation, conservative scope screening, factor provenance and reproducible accounting outputs. Contact: info@transitxlabs.ca.

The [case study](docs/index.md) shows coverage and exclusions; [generated Results](RESULTS.md) provides source-to-result receipts. Measured Canadian validation is planned future work.

See the [current local validation record](docs/validation.md) for tests, coverage and clean-checkout regeneration with declared limits, and [stewardship](docs/stewardship.md) for licence and support scope. No client delivery, remote CI, hosted preview or validated-rightsizing claim is established.

Publish only the sanitized candidate ancestry into an isolated repository, after inspecting all destination refs. Private source history, multi-ref delivery bundles, detailed build/review receipts and historical repair reports are not public release material.
