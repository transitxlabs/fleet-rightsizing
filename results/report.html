Limitations

NRCan factors are standard test-cycle ratings, not municipal duty-cycle measurements. No measured duty-cycle adjustment is available. Inventory and catalogue vintages differ (ICE ratings 2015-2024); neither establishes current inventory or purchase availability.

24 scope/fuel-eligible records lack supported factors, explaining the difference between the catalogue denominator and estimated cohort.

Seattle and Laval inventories lack measured telematics. Utilization uses simulated proxy mileage calibrated from NYC publisher-reported summaries.

Known weights and identified duty tokens screened; specialization remains unresolved. SERVICE BODY and CUTAWAY can pass; both ledgers display unresolved scope/upfit status. Unknown or incompatible factors are excluded; exclusion does not imply zero emissions. Catalogue options are illustrative and awaiting engineering review of payload, body, seating, mission, upfits and current availability.

Emissions factor provenance

NYC calibration is the unweighted median of aggregate model-group miles per vehicle, not a vehicle-weighted duty distribution. Class mappings: SEDAN to Compact; SUV to Sport utility vehicle: Small; VAN to Minivan; TRUCK to Pickup truck: Standard.

Catalogue variant means do not resolve engine/transmission specifications; class proxies are arithmetic means. Generic factors contribute no estimate.

Assumptions and interpretation

The conditional row-bootstrap describes n iid draws from fixed simulated record emissions, with no coverage claim for real finite-fleet emissions. Mileage, calibration and classification errors are omitted.

Transparent assumption range (tonnes): 1,470.0 to 5,539.4. Chosen mileage multipliers 0.7/1.3 times observed factor extrema; no coverage guarantee.

All flags and the utilization chart use the same estimated cohort: 1544 records, including light-duty trucks. Quartiles are recalculated within that cohort. Age flags: 304; kilometre-only flags: 181; quartile flags: 387. Legacy zero fleet scenario values are withheld placeholders; not evaluated fleet outcomes.

Quartile flags do not establish surplus. Measured mileage, dispatch, engine hours, usage frequency, reserve and emergency-response requirements must precede any operational recommendation. Laval is an ingestion case only.

A fictional cost example uses two base vehicles at 12,000 km/year, 960 L gasoline or 1,080 L diesel; BEVs use 20 kWh/100 km. Per-unit CAD capital: 35,000 ICE; 45,000 BEV plus 3,500 charger; no upfits, taxes or residual value. All mileage transfers, so BEV vehicle tailpipe CO2 is zero (100% reduction); lifecycle emissions are excluded.

Two-vehicle cash flows and six-parameter sensitivity are arithmetic illustrations, not municipal fleet savings:
